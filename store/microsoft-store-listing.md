## Submission options → Restricted capabilities

**runFullTrust — why it is needed:**

> Tailzu is a desktop voice keyboard. It listens for a keyboard shortcut from
> any app, records the user's voice while they hold the conversation, and
> types the written result at the cursor in whatever app they are using.
> Meeting notes also hear the computer's sound, with the user's permission.
> These need full desktop access; nothing runs without the user starting it.

### Description

Talk. Tailzu writes.

Tap Ctrl twice and say what you mean. Your words appear at the cursor,
clean and ready to send. No app switching. No copy. No paste.

Say it like you think it. Pause, change your mind, mix languages. Tailzu
drops the filler, keeps your corrections and writes the thought, not the
stumble.

Speak the language in your head. English, Hindi, Hinglish, all 22 Indian
languages and more than 20 others. Switch mid-sentence; Tailzu follows.

Same thought, any voice. Pick how you want to sound, from Professional to
Playful, Concise to Poetic, and every message wears it.

Meeting notes, without taking notes. Press Ctrl+Alt+N and Tailzu listens to
you and the call. When you stop, you get the meeting, a short summary and
the few things that mattered, with who said what.

Works where you already are: email, chat, documents, your browser, ChatGPT,
Claude, your code editor.

Your voice is yours. Audio is deleted once it is written.

One account across your phone and your computer.
